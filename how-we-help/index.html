HOW WE HELP

We don't just show up and deliver. We have some core values that guide how we partner with you.

Our approach

How we partner with you

No blind dates

You want to work with organisations you're aligned with. So do we. Before anything is signed, we work to understand your needs and work out if we're the best fit for you. Not ready for dinner? Let's get a coffee.

With and for you

We are your partner in learning. We don't design in a vacuum. We make sure we understand you so that we can design learning with and for you.

We do the heavy lifting

We're not seagulls. We love accountability.

We don't just architect the learning, we build it, we deliver it and we measure it, giving you time back.

Seriously engaging

We take your satisfaction seriously, and we care about the quality of the learning we deliver. We know 'fun' and 'rigour' are not mutually exclusive. That's why our learning feels seriously engaging.

We work with organisations who know that learning makes a difference. We make that happen.

This means we focus on two things: designing and delivering learning that works, and making sure the right tools are in place to support it.

Learning tools

We won't sell you a drill if all you need is a nail. In fact, we won't try to sell anything at all if it's not needed.

We only help select and implement learning tools that support your strategy and goals, rather than distract from them.

Sometimes clients come to us knowing exactly what they need. Other times, they know what they want to achieve. Either way, we start with your goals and, if relevant, recommend tools that fit your context, not the other way around.

We support organisations to:

  • select learning tools and assistive technologies with confidence
  • create and curate learning content that targets your strategic priorities
  • design beautiful learning and clear pathways
  • use data and reporting to inform decisions

The right tool for the right job, and not just the tools in our warehouse, either.
